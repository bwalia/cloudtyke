Eight design areas, one landing zone

The Cloud Adoption Framework splits a landing zone into eight design areas, A to H. Each area is a set of decisions. Together they shape the platform. Make them in any order you like, but billing, identity and resource organization are the hardest to change later.

In the stadium

The design areas are the stadium plans: the land deal and owner (billing and tenant), stewards and wristbands (identity), the layout of stands (resource organization), gates and walkways (network), security and the control room (security, management), the safety code (governance) and the build crew (automation). Chapter 1 places them in order.
