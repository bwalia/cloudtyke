Watch out for

Moving a subscription changes its rules at once

Role assignments inherited from the old parent disappear, and the new parent's policy applies. Check both before you move.

Deny on day one breaks pipelines

Start with audit (or DoNotEnforce) and read the compliance results. Fix, remediate, add exemptions with an expiry date, then enforce.

Overlapping address spaces

Two VNets with the same range cannot both peer to the hub. Plan an IP scheme first, then re-address the smaller or easier one.

Owners everywhere

Replace standing Owner rights with group-based roles and PIM. Keep two break-glass accounts.

In the stadium

Renovating a stadium while matches still go on: build the new structure around the old one, move stands across one at a time, and post the safety code before you enforce it. See Chapter 3, The safety code.
