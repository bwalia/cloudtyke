Management and security services

Three rules of thumb

One place to look

One central Log Analytics workspace for platform and security data is the usual start. Split only for a clear reason: data residency, very high volume, or separate billing.

On by policy, not by memory

Diagnostic settings, Defender plans, backup and update assessment are switched on by DeployIfNotExists policies. New subscriptions are covered from minute one.

Secrets stay with the app

Each app gets its own Key Vault per environment and region. Never share a vault between apps or between production and test.

Budgets warn, they do not stop

A Cost Management budget sends alerts. To stop spend, use policy (allowed SKUs) or automation on the alert.
