Platform vs application landing zones

A platform landing zone holds shared services that one platform team runs for everyone. An application landing zone is a subscription for one workload. The line between the two teams moves depending on how you choose to manage application landing zones.

Shared responsibility, in plain words

The platform team promises

  • A landing zone that is ready on day one: network, access, policy, logs
  • A working hub, DNS and firewall, with a clear way to ask for rules
  • Guardrails as code, with exemptions that expire
  • Vending that is fast and predictable

The app team promises

  • Build only through code and pipelines
  • Stay inside the guardrails, or ask for an exemption
  • Own the app's security: code, dependencies, data, secrets
  • Watch its own alerts and costs

In the stadium

The platform team is the stadium crew: they build and run the shell, gates, control room and safety code. App teams are event organisers: they bring the show onto the pitch. See the split in Chapter 6, The pitch and the changeover.
