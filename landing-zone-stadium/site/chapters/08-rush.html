Keep the bridge green

Fans arrive by road before kick-off and leave after the final whistle. Try it with no controls first. Then turn the controls on, one by one.

Controls

Three pictures for staying up

The overflow car park must be big enough

If the second region is too small, failover just moves the crowd to a place that also breaks. Reserve the capacity and test it.

RTO is how long until the overflow car park is open. RPO is how many ticket sales you lose in the switch.

Don't let everyone rush at once

After an outage, every client retries at the same moment. Spread them out with backoff and jitter, queues and rate limits.

Open extra gates before kick-off too: scheduled scaling.

Weight limits at the entrance

A limit written on a sign does nothing. Enforce it at the door: rate limits, load shedding with 429 or 503, and a circuit breaker that closes a gate when the crowd behind it is crushing.
